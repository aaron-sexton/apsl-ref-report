Match

1Result and goals

Matches stopped after the 70th minute usually stand as final (Handbook §4.2.6).

Forfeit grounds from Handbook §4.2.7. Enter the score at the time play stopped, if any.

Add every goal with the scorer's number and name – the list must match the final score. For an own goal, pick the team that benefited and enter the number and name of the player who put it in.

2Cards and dismissals

Include coaches and team officials. For a second yellow, pick the reason for the second caution. Any send-off needs a supplemental report (step 8).

3Substitutions

Add every substitution with the number and name of both players. Limit: 7 per team, no re-entry, plus 1 concussion substitute.

4Kickoff & incidents

Tick anything that happened during the match. Items marked describe below need a sentence in the notes box.

5Roster sheets

Photograph each team's marked-up roster sheet: starters, subs with minutes on and off, goals, and cards. Lay it flat, fill the frame, and make sure the writing is readable.
